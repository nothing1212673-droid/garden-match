How to play

  1. Turn over two cards. Each pair of cards counts as one move.
  2. Find a match and both cards stay face up. Different cards turn back over after a short peek.
  3. Match all the cards to move from 4 to 6 to 8 pairs. Finish all three levels to grow your garden.

The timer starts with your first card in each level and pauses between levels. There’s no time limit. Opening these rules does not pause an active timer.

Your best complete game is saved in this browser: fewer moves wins, then a faster time breaks a tie.

Keyboard: Tab or arrow keys to move between cards. Enter or Space to flip. Escape to close these rules.
